In this quickstart you will create a routine that checks your email every weekday morning and lists the messages that are waiting for your reply. It uses a single connector, so you can finish quickly. When it works, you can extend it with the [full workplace guide](/daily_digest_guide)
 
## Before you begin
 
- A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise)
- Claude Desktop installed and signed in
- An email account that Claude can connect to

## Step 1: Turn on an email connector
 
1. Open Claude Desktop and go to **Settings > Connectors**.
2. Enable the connector for your email account, for example Gmail or Microsoft 365 (Outlook).
3. Sign in when asked and approve access.
## Step 2: Create the routine
 
1. Open **Claude Code** in Claude Desktop.
2. Click **Routines** in the left sidebar.
3. Create a new routine and paste the prompt below into the instructions field.
![The New routine screen in Claude Desktop, showing the instructions field, the trigger options, and the Connectors, Behavior and Notifications tabs](/images/routine-setup.png)
 
```text
Every weekday (Monday to Friday) morning, check my email and flag
everything that needs my attention.
 
Search my inbox for the last 24 hours (last 72 hours on Monday, to
cover the weekend) for:
- Emails where I was directly asked something and I have not replied.
- Emails I sent asking someone something, where I never got a reply.
 
List the sender, subject, roughly when, and what is being asked.
Group the output under "Awaiting my reply" and "Waiting on others".
Put the newest and most urgent first. Keep it short.
```
 
## Step 3: Schedule it
 
1. Set the cadence to **Weekdays**.
2. Choose **Cloud** (remote), not local, so it still runs when your laptop is asleep.
3. On the **Notifications** tab, choose how you want to be told when a run finishes.
4. Click **Save**.
## Step 4: Run it once and check the result
 
You do not need to wait until tomorrow morning.
 
1. Open the routine and start a run manually.
2. Open the **Runs** panel and select the run.
3. Read the digest. You should see your unanswered emails grouped under the two headings.
If the digest appears, your routine works. If it is empty or fails, see [Troubleshooting](/daily_digest_guide#troubleshooting).
