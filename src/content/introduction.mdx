Claude Workflow Guides are short, practical guides for automating your daily work with Claude. The first guide shows you how to build a **daily digest routine**: an automated task that runs every weekday morning and tells you what needs your attention.
 
## What is a Claude Routine?
 
A routine is a scheduled task that Claude runs for you. You write the instructions once, choose when it should run, and Claude does the work on its own. The results wait for you in the app.
 
## What does the daily digest do?
 
Each weekday morning, the routine checks your work tools and builds a short summary of:
 
- Messages and emails that ask you something and still have no reply
- Commitments you made that do not look finished yet
- Tickets that are due today or soon
- Other items worth knowing about, such as mentions in documents
It works as a second pair of eyes, so fewer things slip through the cracks.
 
## Who is this for?
 
These guides are for people who split their day across email, chat, and a ticket tracker, and who want a clear morning summary instead of checking every tool by hand. You do not need to write code.
 
## What you need
 
- A paid Claude plan (Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise)
- Claude Desktop
- At least one connector turned on in **Settings > Connectors**
